Use this in a separate chat. Read [02_eo12_cso.md](../02_eo12_cso.md) and [03_sco.md](../03_sco.md) first.

## Question

Take the flags in `ops_anomalies` and say which of the four levers each one resembles: more efficient delivery, consolidating programs, insourcing, or sunsetting. Where the public row does not support a lever, say so and leave it as a question.

## Rules

- Public data and the published order only. Do not commit `data/`, `.env`, or secrets.
- Do not invent a savings dollar, and do not treat the Executive Budget's $1.77 billion as a result of this analysis.
- The pipeline already stores a lever on each flag. Check it. Correct it in a finding when the label overreaches.
- Write `knowledge/findings/cso.md` with one short paragraph per flag you keep: the agency, the public movement, the lever, and the question for the six-month update.

Overtime flags should stay on efficient delivery unless a public source says the driver is a contract or a program that should end.
