# Knowledge card: Strategic Coordination and Operations

**In one line:** the team that helps agencies carry out mayoral priorities, Executive Order 12, and citywide operational reforms, including where a saving and a service have to be true at the same time.

## What the work is

The Mayor's Management Report says what an agency spent and what it delivered. Executive Order 12 asks every agency to find recurring savings and to weigh them against the programs that produce results. Someone has to hold those two views together across agencies, on a deadline, without each Chief Savings Officer inventing a private scoreboard.

That is the job of this pane. It is a management draft, built only from public rows:

- Citywide, which agencies account for FY2026 spending, and which of them carry a flag.
- For one agency, five years of expenditures, overtime, and personnel.
- For one goal, the financial plan against prior-year spending, and the indicators under that goal.
- A short list of indicators worth putting in the next six-month update, chosen because they moved against the aim the City already published.

## What it will not do

- It will not estimate a savings dollar. A flag is a question to take to the agency.
- It will not treat a flat budget as success if the indicators moved the wrong way, and it will not treat a spending increase as failure if the indicators moved the right way.
- It will not use private agency files, internal headcount systems, or credentials. If a conversation needs those, they stay outside this repo.

## Where to put new research

Write a new card in this folder. Name the dataset, the column, and the fiscal year. If a figure came from the pipeline, say which script. Do not paste raw extracts into git.
